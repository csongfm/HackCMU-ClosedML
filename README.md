# briefly

briefly turns a users's unique interests, knowledge levels, and historic preferences into a curated selection of news articles and a 5, 10, or 20 minute audio news briefing.

Developed by Ekaansh Agrawal, Lakshya Wahi, Cheren Song, and Devon Parikh as a submission to the Optimization Track of the 2026 HackCMU 24-hr hackathon.
